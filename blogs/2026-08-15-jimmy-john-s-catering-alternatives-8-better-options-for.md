Jimmy John's maps a tight delivery zone around every store, so plenty of offices fall outside it before they ever pick a sandwich. The catering menu is also cold from end to end, runs on one bread, and arrives with nobody to set it up. Here are 8 Jimmy John's catering alternatives for the office, from wider-radius sub shops and boxed lunches to hot buffets, sandwich bars, and mezze spreads.
