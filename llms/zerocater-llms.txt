# Zerocater

> Zerocater provides corporate catering and office food programs, helping companies build a better workplace through food. Our AI ordering product, CaterAi, recommends and manages catering based on a team's preferences and order history. We serve 12+ major U.S. metros.

## Products & Solutions
- [CaterAi](https://zerocater.com/cater-ai/): AI-powered catering ordering and recommendations.
- [Corporate Catering](https://zerocater.com/solutions/corporate-catering/): Office catering and recurring food programs.
- [Corporate Cafeterias](https://zerocater.com/solutions/corporate-cafeterias/): Managed on-site cafeteria programs.
- [Event Catering](https://zerocater.com/solutions/corporate-event-catering/): Catering for corporate events and gatherings.
